## Scope and method

The search now uses the supplied bibliography as a set of leads rather than as the complete list. It traverses the entire paginated [Noormags author catalogue](https://www.noormags.ir/view/fa/creator/11958/) (62 records across seven pages), checks the separate [غ ع راقم catalogue](https://www.noormags.ir/view/fa/creator/64938/), and checks original Roshd publisher contents and transcripts. Every one of the 62 author-catalogue entries was fetched for citation metadata, and new multi-person entries were checked for contributor roles.

Useful search forms include غلامرضا عمرانی، غلام‌رضا عمرانی، غلام رضا عمرانی، غلامرضا عمراني، غ ع راقم، راقم غ ع; distinctive article titles; serial issue numbers; and column names such as زنگار بر آیینه، معلمان خوب من، خاطرات و تجربه‌ها. Publisher bylines and full texts help distinguish participation from authorship.

Medical and architecture records attributed to people with the same name were excluded: name matching alone is insufficient. Editorial-board membership alone is also insufficient. Multiple catalogue entries with the same title are compared by venue, issue, date, pages and contributor roles before being treated as duplicates.

This is a documented search pass, not a claim that the archive is exhaustive. Catalogue metadata remains distinct from inspection of printed scans. Exact day precision in catalogue dates should not be inferred for seasonal issues.
